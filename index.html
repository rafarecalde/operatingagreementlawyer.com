Governance Structures We Draft

We tailor Operating Agreements to reflect your business’s ownership and decision-making structure—whether lean or complex.

Member-Managed

Day-to-Day Control by Owners

Ideal for closely held companies where all members participate in operations and decision-making. Authority is shared, and all major decisions require member action.

Manager-Managed

Delegated Authority

Centralizes decision-making in designated managers, allowing passive members to retain ownership without operational control. Often used for investor-backed or family-run LLCs.

Board-Style Governance

Committees & Supermajority Triggers

For more sophisticated entities: we include board-style oversight, supermajority voting thresholds, deadlock protections, and layered consent rights. Built for scale and control.
